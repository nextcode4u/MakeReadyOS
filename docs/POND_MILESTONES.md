# Frog Pond Team Milestones

Team credit belongs to a property, not an individual or a speed leaderboard.
Milestones are earned at 1, 5, 10, 25, 50, and 100 approved turns. Counts continue
after the final milestone. Personal outfits and discoveries remain separate and
retain their existing browser-local storage and unlock rules.

## Counting

- Only new normal final-walk sign-offs count. Readiness checks still apply.
- No historical backfill, imported-ready credit, or administrative-override credit.
- Each turn record counts once, even after reopening, reapproval, archiving, or
  deletion. A new turn record for a later vacancy can earn new credit.
- Completion credit and milestone notifications commit with sign-off. A property
  lock serializes concurrent approvals; a unique turn key prevents duplicate credit.
- Counts are server-saved and independent of loaded board rows or Pond visits.
- The read API returns only accessible active properties. It is session-only.

## Notifications

Only milestone thresholds generate celebrations, not every completed turn.
Active administrators and property-scoped managers, technicians, painters,
cleaners, and leasing receive shared recognition. Existing work alerts remain.
The separate Team milestones category respects global/property preferences and
quiet hours. Need-to-know only also mutes celebrations. No push delivery is queued.
View in Pond opens the relevant property. Muting notices does not remove credit.

## Verification

Focused tests cover milestone thresholds, duplicate credit, scoped recipients,
muting, in-app-only delivery, final-walk approval/reapproval, imported and override
exclusions, access checks, notification navigation, and mobile/desktop layout.
